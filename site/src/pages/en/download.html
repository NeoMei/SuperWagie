10 November 2026

Apple silicon Mac · Windows x64

Sandbox recharge, simulated settlement and sandbox-only offers are removed from the billing page. Purchases use an existing authorized balance or a real WeChat/Alipay checkout. Unresolved sandbox recharge records no longer block a new formal purchase.

Usage summaries put credits and call counts first. Cash, quota and nonzero refunds stay under Billing details in Consumption records. Credit amounts retain precision and omit extra trailing zeros.

Memory can recover after a startup failure and reports unavailable until startup succeeds. This release fixes experience searches and learning or memory organization tasks that could remain queued. Conversation learning still requires opt-in. Read about memory issues.

A declined refund keeps the fulfilled purchase and explicitly shows that no refund occurred.

The user center combines a large avatar sidebar with top navigation for membership, API keys, usage, orders and personal details.

Daily platform call totals use UTC dates and are saved in the background across restarts. Initial history fills in batches across the past 365 days. Counts have no 5,000-record scan cap; dates without saved observations remain unknown. Numeric cards separate total, subscription, add-on and other remaining credits.
