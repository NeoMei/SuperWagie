About SuperWagie

An AI workbench for office work, built local-first.

What it is

SuperWagie puts the common parts of office work in one app: what needs doing today, where your own material lives, and how documents and decks get finished. It is not a chat window with a few buttons; the workbench, the library and creation run as one desktop workspace.

It is also a general-purpose and coding agent: it reads a codebase, runs commands, shows Git changes and writes tests, and long tasks are planned and checked on its own. Office work and code share one workspace, so nothing has to be moved between two apps.

Tasks and notes are ordinary files, the library index sits on your disk, and you bring your own model key. The app has no account and does not require uploading files to a cloud first.

Third-party components

The bundled library, presentation, document, video and web skills come from different projects; their sources and notices ship with the app and are recorded in the repository’s NOTICE.
